# 🏥 Clinic Appointment System

A modern web-based **Clinic Appointment System** designed to simplify and manage daily healthcare operations in one platform.

The system helps clinics manage patients, appointments, doctors, billing, laboratory services, inventory, staff, telemedicine, and other healthcare activities efficiently.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📅 Appointment Management
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Management
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Patient Management
- 💳 Billing & Payments
- 🩸 Blood Bank Management
- 🧪 Laboratory Management
- 💊 Inventory Management
- 🏥 Hospital Management
- 📋 Patient Reports
- 👥 Staff & User Management
- 📞 Emergency Services
- 💻 Telemedicine
- 🗺️ Clinic Map
- 🔐 User Login & Settings
- 📊 Dashboard & Statistics
