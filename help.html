    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I register a new patient?
    Navigate to Patients Management and click the "Register New Patient" button. Fill in the required details including name, phone, age, and gender. Click Submit to save the patient record.
    How do I book an appointment?
    Go to the Appointments page and click "Book Appointment". Select a registered patient, choose a doctor, and pick a date and time. The appointment will appear in the queue.
    How do I create an invoice?
    Visit Billing & Finance and click "Create New Invoice". Select the patient, enter the service details, amount, and payment method. The invoice will be generated instantly.
    How do I change the system language?
    Use the language dropdown in the top header of any page. You can choose between English, Somali, and Arabic. The system supports full RTL (Right-to-Left) layout for Arabic.
    Is my data secure?
    Yes. ClinicCare Pro uses 256-bit encryption, two-factor authentication (2FA via SMS), and Face ID verification. All sessions are monitored through the Audit Logs module.

    Expanded Q&A Mode

    Enable Expanded Q&A mode to allow longer AI conversation history (up to 100 question/answer pairs). Use only under clinician oversight — this mode may provide more detailed guidance but does not replace professional medical advice.

    When enabled, the AI will retain up to 100 recent question/answer pairs in browser storage to improve context. All data is stored locally in your browser.
